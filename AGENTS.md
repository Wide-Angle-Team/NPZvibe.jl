# Instructions for AI Agents

## Commit Messages

- Use the imperative mood in the subject line (e.g. "Add feature" not "Added feature").
- Keep the subject line under 72 characters.
- Separate subject from body with a blank line.
- Always append a Co-Authored-By line as the last line of every commit message. Replace `<harness>` with your harness name, `<agent>` with your agent or model identifier, and `<email>` with the appropriate contact address:

```
Co-Authored-By: <harness> <agent> <<email>>
```
